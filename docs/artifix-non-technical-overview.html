1. Executive Summary

Artifix is a verified marketplace and digital escrow platform built to resolve the fundamental trust deficit in the skilled home and construction trades (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, solar technicians, painters, and HVAC contractors).

In emerging markets—and specifically across Nigeria—hiring skilled artisans is plagued by chronic friction:

  • Property owners and homeowners are forced to make large upfront cash advances to unverified workers, frequently suffering from abandoned jobs, substandard materials, or disappeared artisans.
  • Artisans and craftsmen routinely face unpaid labor, endless haggling after work completion, or delayed payments that jeopardize their livelihood.

Artifix eliminates this risk through Milestone-Based Escrow. Instead of direct cash handovers, client funds are securely locked into an automated digital vault. Artisans start work knowing their pay is 100% guaranteed, and clients release funds only after inspecting verifiable, photographic proof of completed deliverables.

💡 The Core Philosophy: Complete Symmetry of Trust
Artifix removes the need for blind trust. Clients never pay upfront in full without recourse, and artisans never work on empty promises.

3. The 4-Step Customer Journey

Artifix transforms a stressful, unstructured process into an intuitive four-step workflow designed for everyday people:

Step 01
Scope & Milestones

Client and verified artisan agree on precise deliverables, material specifications, and clear project phases on a binding digital docket.

Step 02
Lock in Escrow

Client deposits milestone funds via local bank card, bank transfer (Naira), or digital currency into a tamper-proof digital vault.

Step 03
Proof of Work

Artisan performs work on site and uploads timestamped completion photos, test video logs, and notes to the job docket.

Step 04
Inspect & Payout

Client inspects and approves the deliverable. Escrow instantly disburses funds directly to the artisan's commercial bank account.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Do I need any cryptocurrency or technical knowledge to use Artifix?
No. Over 85% of Artifix clients fund milestones using their regular Nigerian bank debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve) or instant bank transfers via Paystack. The secure escrow logic operates seamlessly in the background without any crypto or wallet setup required.
Q: What happens if an artisan does substandard work or abandons a project?
Your deposited funds remain 100% safe in the escrow vault. The artisan cannot access the money. If an issue occurs, a 48-hour cure window gives the artisan an opportunity to rectify the defect. If unresolved, neutral Artifix trade arbiters review the photos and site logs against the contract scope to disburse a prompt refund directly to your original payment method.
Q: How do artisans get paid, and how fast is payout settlement?
Settlement happens instantly the moment the client inspects and approves a completed milestone. Funds are transferred immediately to the artisan's commercial bank account (GTBank, Access, Zenith, First Bank, Kuda, etc.) with zero holding delays or withdrawal fees.
Q: Can Artifix be used for small quick repairs as well as major multi-month construction?
Yes. Artifix supports single-milestone "Rapid Fix" contracts for urgent plumbing leaks, generator repairs, or electrical troubleshooting, as well as complex multi-milestone contracts for large residential renovations, roof installation, and full solar setups.
Q: How does Artifix verify its artisans?
Every verified artisan completes our comprehensive 4-step accreditation: (1) National Identity (NIN and verified biometric match), (2) Physical workshop or residential address inspection, (3) Practical trade competency and previous work audit by senior guild examiners, and (4) Background screening.
