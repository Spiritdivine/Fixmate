1. Executive Summary & Problem Space

In trade services and home repairs, non-technical clients face acute barriers before an artisan is booked: lack of technical vocabulary, fear of overcharging, and frequent misdiagnoses leading to wrong artisan dispatches. The Artifix AI Copilot transforms Fixmate into an intelligent triage clinic.

  • Linguistic Deficit: Homeowners describe symptoms ("ac dey sweat") rather than root causes ("blocked condensate drain").
  • Misdiagnosis: Electrician called for compressor mechanics, resulting in wasted transit fees and delays.
  • Price Asymmetry: Lack of objective insight into fair local labor rates and actual spare parts costs.

💡 The Artifix Solution

  • Dual-Track Output: Immediate safety steps + price radar for clients, paired with automated job briefs for artisans.
  • 5-Pillar Matching: Ranked dispatch based on GPS distance, Bayesian rating, KYC, and portfolio similarity.

2. Multimodal Ingestion Pipeline

2.1 Voice Note Acoustic Architecture

Voice is the primary communication mode across West Africa. Clients record audio via the browser MediaRecorder API (compressed to 32kbps mono Opus in WebM containers). The audio buffer streams to Gemini 2.0 / Whisper primed with an extensive Nigerian trade terminology dictionary (e.g., "pumping machine don knock", "nepaa took light", "gen dey surge", "ac dey sweat").

2.2 Computer Vision Photo Pipeline

The client can attach up to 4 photos (wide shot, close-up of defect, serial/model plate, context). Before upload, the frontend compresses images to max 1920x1080 WebP. Cloudinary returns secure signed URLs which are fed directly into the Gemini multimodal vision endpoint.

2.3 Interactive Clarification Loop (Conversational Triage)

When the confidence score is below 70%, the AI pauses before matching and outputs 1–2 quick-tap multiple choice questions (e.g., "Is the compressor fan spinning outside?" [Yes] [No] [Cannot Access]).

⚠️ Real-Time Hazard Detection Protocol
When severity is marked as CRITICAL and isHazardous === true (active electrical arcing, gas smell, structural ceiling crack), the system immediately displays a high-visibility Emergency Alert Banner with step-by-step containment steps before any quotes or artisan matches are rendered.

4. Intelligent Artisan Matching Algorithm

Every candidate artisan in the target trade category is scored on a normalized 0 to 100 scale using the 5-pillar mathematical formulation:

Final Score Formula:
Score(a) = (0.35 × DistScore) + (0.25 × RatingScore) + (0.20 × PortfolioScore) + (0.10 × KycScore) + (0.10 × VelocityScore)
  • Distance Score (35%): Linear decay over a 20km radius using Haversine GPS distance. Artisans within 3km receive maximum points.
  • Bayesian Rating (25%): Normalizes ratings against review volume: (v × R + 5 × 3.5) / (v + 5) × 20, preventing zero-review 5-star bias.
  • Portfolio Similarity (20%): Evaluates semantic match between diagnostic failure mode and verified portfolio tags.
  • KYC Verification (10%): 100 points for approved government identity verification (NIN/BVN), 0 points for unverified accounts.
  • Completion Velocity (10%): Reward for historical low dispute ratio and prompt contract turnaround.

6. Strategic Extra Features to Elevate the Platform

1. Artisan Prep Dossier & Pre-BOM

Generates an automated tool/part checklist so the artisan brings the correct capacitor or valve on Trip #1, eliminating the notorious 3-hour inspection market delay.

2. Anti-Gouging Fair Price Radar

Displays an objective median labor price band derived from Fixmate's historical transaction ledger for the client's specific LGA/City.

3. Two-Way Multimodal Audio Bridge

Converts client's formal problem statement into a 25s localized voice note in Nigerian Pidgin sent to the artisan's WhatsApp / PWA.

4. AI Post-Repair Before/After Verification

Compares pre-repair fault photos with post-repair completion photos before authorizing escrow milestone payout release.

5. Emergency 1-Tap SOS Dispatch

Bypasses normal 3-bid proposals when hazards are detected. Simultaneously pings the 3 closest emergency on-call artisans.

6. Interactive Visual AR Photo Annotation

Overlays glowing bounding boxes on the uploaded photo: "Turn this blue valve 90 degrees clockwise to stop the leak."
